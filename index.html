Theoretical Formulation: Stochastic Renewal & Survival Models

1. Stochastic Multi-Stage Renewal Process: Patent prosecution pendency time $T$ is governed by a sequential renewal process across state spaces: Filing $\to$ Publication $\to$ First Office Action (FOA) $\to$ Applicant Response $\to$ Final Action / RCE $\to$ Allowance $\to$ Patent Issuance.

$$h(t) = \frac{k}{\lambda} \left( \frac{t}{\lambda} \right)^{k-1}, \quad S(t) = \exp\left( -\left(\frac{t}{\lambda}\right)^k \right)$$
Where $\lambda$ represents the scale parameter (calibrated to Art Unit docket backlogs) and $k$ represents the shape parameter ($k > 1$ models docket wear-in / increasing review hazard).

2. Monte Carlo Stochastic Sampling: For each simulation run $i \in \{1, \dots, N\}$, random Weibull duration variables are sampled via inverse transform:

$$T_i = \lambda_{\text{eff}} \cdot \left( -\ln(1 - U_i) \right)^{1/k}, \quad U_i \sim \mathcal{U}(0, 1)$$
With effective scale $\lambda_{\text{eff}} = \lambda_{\text{baseline}} \cdot \phi_{\text{track}} \cdot \phi_{\text{examiner}} \cdot (1 + \omega_{\text{claims}})$.

3. Statutory Fee Schedule Formulation: Total official PTO fees are calculated according to 37 CFR 1.16 & 1.18:

$$\text{Cost}_{\text{PTO}} = \beta_{\text{entity}} \cdot \left[ F_{\text{base}} + \max(0, C_{\text{indep}} - 3) \cdot F_{\text{indep}} + \max(0, C_{\text{total}} - 20) \cdot F_{\text{total}} + \mathbb{I}_{\text{Track1}} \cdot F_{\text{Track1}} + N_{\text{RCE}} \cdot F_{\text{RCE}} + F_{\text{issue}} \right]$$
